# Mofu terms of use — DRAFT

> **Draft for legal review. Not approved, not legal advice.** Intended domain: **mofu.lol**. Mofu was previously branded Orbit; existing Solidity artifact names and internal identifiers may retain that name.

## Product facts counsel needs

- Mofu's browser interface is non-custodial: users retain their keys and approve wallet transactions.
- **Launches:** users choose token metadata; Mofu does not endorse tokens or prevent impersonation. The launch contract is unaudited. Its configured trading fee goes to the deployment's fee recipient. No mainnet launch deployment has been broadcast during the branding update.
- **Swap / Earn / Borrow:** existing protocols are accessed through Circle App Kit and third-party Morpho markets/vaults. Mofu currently configures no additional App Kit fee; provider/protocol fees still apply.
- **Public profiles and logos:** user-supplied metadata is published through Pinata/IPFS and may remain accessible permanently. Wallet authorization does not verify a user's real-world identity or their claims.
- **Points:** derived from public trading volume; not redeemable, not a token, and not a promise of a reward.
- **Fund-manager agent:** separate open-source tooling acting under the wallet authority and limits its operator supplies.
- **Admin powers:** the configured launch owner can pause new launches and change the fee recipient. The launch contract cannot pause curve trading or withdraw graduated LP liquidity. Production ownership is intended to use a multisig; a proposed hackathon deployment may instead use an explicitly disclosed single-wallet owner and fee recipient. That is experimental, not equivalent security.
- **Risks:** smart-contract bugs, liquidation, changing rates/liquidity, frozen assets, provider failures, and unrecoverable loss of funds remain possible. Hackathon status does not remove these risks.
