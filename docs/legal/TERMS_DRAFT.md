# Terms of use — DRAFT

> **Draft for legal review. Not approved, not legal advice.** Key product facts are listed so counsel can draft accurately.

## Product facts counsel needs

- Orbit is a non-custodial interface. It never holds user keys or funds; users sign every transaction in their own wallet.
- **Launches:** anyone can create a token on Orbit's launch contract. Orbit does not review, endorse or list projects; token names may imitate real brands. A 1% fee on each curve trade goes to the protocol fee recipient (a multisig). The contract is unaudited until the audit gate in `docs/MAINNET_PLAN.md` closes.
- **Swap / Earn / Borrow:** routed through Circle App Kit and third-party Morpho vaults. Orbit takes no fee on these today.
- **Points:** derived from public volume; not a token, not redeemable, no promise of value. Orbit may change or end them.
- **Fund-manager agent:** open-source tooling users run themselves. It acts only through wallets and limits the user configures.
- **Admin powers:** the multisig can pause *new* launches and change the fee recipient. It cannot pause trading, move user funds, or withdraw graduated liquidity.
